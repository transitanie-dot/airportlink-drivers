Questions partners ask

01

No rides are showing. Why?

Four possible reasons, and the Available rides page tells you which one: your account is not approved yet, you have not chosen any airports, you have no payout details, or there is genuinely nothing at your airports right now.

02

How and when am I paid?

Once a month. At the start of each month you receive a statement covering every ride you completed in the previous one, and the money goes to the account on your payouts page. The fee shown on each ride is what reaches you in full.

03

I took a ride and cannot do it.

Release it from the Available rides page — possible until 24 hours before pick-up. Inside 24 hours, call us. Do not simply not turn up: there is a passenger at an airport counting on you.

04

The passenger is not at the pick-up point.

Call the number on the ride. If there is no answer, wait and call us — we have the flight details and can often find out what happened.

05

A document of mine expired.

Rides stop reaching you on the expiry date, and we warn you 30 days, 7 days and 1 day before. Upload the new one and you are back in immediately. Rides you already accepted are not cancelled — please still do them.

06

Can I add more drivers and vehicles later?

Any time, from the Drivers and Vehicles pages. A bigger vehicle means bigger groups reach you: a booking for six only appears to companies with a vehicle that seats six.

07

Do you take a commission?

No. The fee on the offer is transferred to you in full. Our margin is in what the passenger pays, which is a separate number you never have to think about.
